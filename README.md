🚀 CareerPilot – Career Discovery & Guidance Website

CareerPilot is a simple, interactive career guidance website designed to help students explore different career opportunities, identify required skills, and understand possible career paths.

🌟 Features

- Career Discovery: Explore careers based on interests, skills, and hobbies.
- Skill Gap Analyzer: Compare your current skills with the skills required for different careers.
- Career Roadmap: Get beginner-friendly steps to start exploring a selected career.
- Career Simulation: Try sample tasks related to different professions.
- Career Guidance Chat: Get predefined answers to common career-related questions.

💼 Career Categories

- Technology and Engineering
- Medical and Healthcare
- Business and Management
- Content Creation and Media
- Education and Teaching
- Politics and Public Service
- Communication and Public Speaking
- Arts and Design
